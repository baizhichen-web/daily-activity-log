## 材料

第一章开篇第一段（181 词 / 6 句），原文按句编号：

- **S1** This book is a posthumanist exploration of design.
- **S2** The arc in design of the last thirty to forty years has been to conceptualize, attend to, and prioritize human values.
- **S3** This arc, represented in a series of paradigms that includes human factors, ergonomics, human cognition, embodied interaction, cooperative work, human experience, human ethics, and labor, among others, has led to a greater social and behavioral understanding of what it is to be human.
- **S4** Humanist design or what is commonly referred to as human-centered design, has been overwhelmingly successful for some in shaping technologies to human needs and desires in matters of safety, health, well-being, pleasure, learning, and convenience.
- **S5** While this success has not been evenly distributed, the promise of success has.
- **S6** As a matter of capitalism, this promise arguably exploits through cheap labor and poor working conditions; as a matter of consumerism it manipulates others through ideas of consumption and productivity as the paths to well-being; or as a matter of idealism it fosters a naïveté in which the universality of good design will eventually be accessible to all through human progress.

## 逐句分析

### S1
- 主干：This book / is / a posthumanist exploration of design
- 挂件：`of design` 修饰 exploration
- 点掉：posthumanist 后人类主义的（全书立场词）
- 作用：主题句

### S2
- 主干：The arc / has been / to conceptualize, attend to, and prioritize human values（不定式作表语）
- 挂件：`in design` 限定领域；`of the last thirty to forty years` 限定整条主线的时间跨度
- 点掉：arc 熟词僻义＝发展脉络；attend to＝关注、致力于（非"参加"）；三个动词共用宾语 human values
- 作用：给时间范围、抛主线

### S3
- 主干：This arc / has led to / a greater social and behavioral understanding
- 挂件：`represented in a series of paradigms...` 过去分词短语插在主谓之间，修饰 This arc；`that includes...` 定从修饰 paradigms；`of what it is to be human` 修饰 understanding，其中 what it is to be human 是名词性从句
- 点掉：among others＝以及诸如此类（属范式清单）；what it is to be human 非疑问句
- 作用：主线的表现与后果

### S4
- 主干：Humanist design or what is ...（主）/ has been / overwhelmingly successful
- 挂件：`or what is commonly referred to as human-centered design` 与主语并列（A or B，B 为名词性从句）；`for some` 限定范围（为 S5 埋线）；`in shaping...` 修饰 successful；`in matters of...` 限定领域
- 点掉：design 后逗号是插入造成；shape A to B＝把 A 塑造得符合 B
- 作用：让步，先承认成绩

### S5
- 主干：the promise of success / has [been distributed]
- 挂件：`While...` 让步状语从句
- 点掉：句尾省略 been distributed；promise 熟词僻义＝前景；this success → the promise of success 是全段枢纽
- 作用：转折枢轴

### S6
- 主干：三个分句并列（分号），主语依次 this promise / it / it
- 挂件：三个 `As a matter of X` 作分句状语；`arguably` 软化；`as the paths to well-being` 是 ideas 的补足语；`in which...` 定从修饰 a naïveté
- 点掉：exploits 此处不及物；naïveté＝天真；universality＝普遍性
- 作用：展开批评（三个层面）

## 总结

### 关键词表

熟词僻义：attend to（关注/致力于 ≠ 参加）、arc（发展脉络 ≠ 弧线，已收）、promise（前景 ≠ 承诺，已收）、exploits（不及物用法）
学术通用：arguably、fosters、universality、accessible；已收：overwhelmingly、conceptualize、prioritize、manipulates
领域术语：paradigm、human factors、cooperative work、consumerism、naïveté；已收：posthumanist、ergonomics、embodied、cognition、ethics

### 难点句索引

S3（主谓被长插入语劈开）、S4（or 并列主语 + 两层 in）、S5（句尾省略）、S6（三分号并列 + in which 从句）

### 全文翻译

- **S1** 本书是对设计的后人类主义探索。
- **S2** 过去三四十年间，设计的发展脉络一直是去概念化、关注并优先考虑人的价值。
- **S3** 这条脉络体现为一系列范式——其中包括人因、人体工程学、人类认知、具身交互、协同工作、人类经验、人类伦理以及劳动，等等——它已经带来了对社会与行为层面的"人之为人"的更深入理解。
- **S4** 人本设计——或者说通常被称为以人为中心的设计——对某些人而言，在把技术塑造得符合人的需求与欲望这件事上取得了压倒性的成功，具体涉及安全、健康、福祉、愉悦、学习与便利。
- **S5** 尽管这种成功并未被均等地分配，但成功的前景却得到了均等的分配。
- **S6** 从资本主义的角度看，这一前景可以说是通过廉价劳动和恶劣的工作条件来剥削的；从消费主义的角度看，它通过把消费与生产力视为通往福祉之路的观念来操纵他人；或者，从理想主义的角度看，它滋养出一种天真：在这种天真里，好设计的普遍性终将随着人类进步而为所有人所及。
