# Lead Engagement Manager, FDE - Healthcare & Life Sciences

CSQ227R171

The Lead Engagement Manager delivers measurable impact for our Healthcare & Life Sciences customers as well as at the Business Unit (BU) or Regional team level in the Forward Deployed Engineering organization. You will own and independently scope multi-quarter, highly complex initiatives to solve problems with no clear precedent, operating with high autonomy in high-stakes environments.

The impact you will have:

Regional Impact: Drive excellence at the BU/Region level, contributing to team-level booking targets and consumption goals.

Complex Problem Solving: Navigate complex regional, regulatory, or market-specific complexities across multiple countries.

Mentorship: Coach and support junior Engagement Managers, acting as a go-to resource for peers on complex projects.

Sales Strategy: Shape account strategy to align with client goals and lead the sales of complex services and training offerings.

Strategic Relationships: Establish and maintain strategic relationships with senior customer stakeholders across multiple engagements.

What we look for:

10+ years of leadership experience in Professional Services.

Expert-level understanding of Professional Services & Training offerings and the ability to customize solutions for complex environments.

Proficient thought leadership, increasingly viewed as an external expert in the industry.

Participate in the hiring process to attract top-tier talent. 

Expertise in Databricks architecture, ecosystem, and strategic account planning.

Ability to travel up to 25-50% as required.
