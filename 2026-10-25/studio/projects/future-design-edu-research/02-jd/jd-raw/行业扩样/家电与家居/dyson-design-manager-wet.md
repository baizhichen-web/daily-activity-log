Design Manager - Wet
Summary
Salary: CompetitiveJob Family: Design EngineeringLocation: China - Shanghai Office
About Dyson

Dyson is a global technology company focused on solving the problems others choose to ignore. Our engineers, scientists and designers develop pioneering technologies across a growing portfolio of products for global markets.

China plays an important role in Dyson’s global product development, engineering and manufacturing network. The China team works closely with Dyson’s international Research, Design and Development teams, as well as local engineering, supplier and manufacturing partners, to support the development and delivery of innovative Floorcare products for global markets.

About the Role

Reporting to Senior Design Manager, the Design Manager is responsible for leading the technical delivery of Floorcare development programmes within Dyson’s Research, Design and Development organisation in China.

The role provides engineering leadership across cross-functional teams, ensuring projects are delivered to Dyson’s standards of quality, performance, reliability and innovation. Acting as a key interface between Dyson’s global RDD organisation and China-based development, supplier and manufacturing partners, the Design Manager supports programme execution, technical decision-making and capability development within the China team.

Key Responsibilities

Lead the delivery of Floorcare development programmes from concept through industrialisation and production readiness.
Act as the primary technical point of contact for assigned projects, coordinating activities across Engineering, Operations, Quality, Procurement and Programme Management teams.
Drive alignment between China-based development activities and Dyson’s global engineering standards, processes and governance.
Work closely with project managers and technical leads to define development plans, resource requirements and delivery priorities.
Lead technical reviews, risk assessments and structured problem-solving activities to ensure project milestones are achieved.
Collaborate with engineering, supplier and manufacturing partners in China to support product development, validation and industrialisation activities.
Manage technical risks, issues and dependencies, escalating where required to maintain programme delivery.
Translate high-level product requirements into actionable development plans and cross-functional workstreams.
Ensure engineering solutions meet the required standards for product performance, safety, quality, reliability, cost and manufacturability.
Support capability building within the China development organisation through coaching, knowledge sharing and technical leadership.
Represent Engineering during programme reviews, milestone assessments and stakeholder discussions.
Promote effective collaboration between the China team, Dyson’s global engineering teams and external partners.
About You

Degree in Engineering, Product Design, Science or a related technical discipline.
Significant experience in product design and development within a technology, engineering or consumer products environment.
Strong understanding of end-to-end product development, including design, validation, tooling, industrialisation and manufacturing processes.
Experience working with cross-functional teams and managing complex technical projects.
Demonstrated ability to provide technical leadership and guide engineering teams through development challenges.
Ability to influence and collaborate effectively across international teams and external partners.
Experience working with China-based engineering, supplier or manufacturing partners would be advantageous.
Strong communication, stakeholder management and problem-solving skills.
Commercial awareness, with the ability to balance technical excellence, cost, quality and delivery objectives.
A proactive and structured approach to managing technical risks, priorities and programme dependencies.
