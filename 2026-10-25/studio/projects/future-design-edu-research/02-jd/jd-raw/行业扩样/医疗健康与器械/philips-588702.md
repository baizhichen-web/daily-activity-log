Job Description
Product Designer (Bothell, WA)

As a Product Designer working on Philips Oral Healthcare’s solutions, you are part of a global multidisciplinary design team and will be responsible for designing oral care products and experiences that will improve people’s lives. You will assume product design responsibilities from consulting, framing the design challenge, planning the design approach, ideation, concept, specification to seeing the design through to production and launch. This role is focused on the design of physical consumer products and hardware experiences.

Your role:

Translate commercial briefs into user needs, pain points, and design opportunities. Monitor product trends and competitive landscape to inform direction. Frame the design challenge and design the project approach.
Define design problems, success criteria, and requirements; generate ideas through sketching and ideation, and evolve them into cohesive concepts (form, color, material, finish, interaction). Define critical design requirements as input to development team.
Create experience flow to understand the user experience journey and opportunities for the product and solution. Partner with engineering, interaction, packaging, and global design teams to ensure usability, feasibility, and alignment from concept through production.
Create high-quality renderings, presentations, and specifications (e.g., 3D CAD, CMF) and effectively communicate concepts to stakeholders. Review and validate designs through critiques, engineering builds, and samples; ensure final outputs meet design intent, maintain documentation, and follow development processes.
Guide junior designers and agency partners to achieve the desired project outcome.
Lead design project evaluation to capture learnings for continuous improvements.

You’re the right fit if:

You’ve acquired 3+ years of working experience in Product Design or Industrial Design. Consumer electronics experience required.
Your skills include:
You have a strong affinity for designing with form, color, material, and finishing.
You collaborate effectively with other functions to solve problems and achieve goals under tight timelines.
You are able to manage and lead design projects independently with minimal supervision.
You are proficient with 3D Computer aided design (CAD) software such as Rhino, CREO, SolidWorks or similar, and rendering tools such as Key Shot, or similar.
You have a Bachelor’s/Master’s Degree in (UX) Design, Interaction Design, Visual Design, Computer Science Engineering or equivalent; OR 7+ years of above related experience.
You must be able to successfully perform the following minimum Physical, Cognitive and Environmental job requirements with or without accommodation for this position.
You have strong written and verbal communication skills. You are creative and empathetic, enabling you to create appropriate solutions that solve business and customer needs. You have a positive outlook, are hardworking and adaptable.
