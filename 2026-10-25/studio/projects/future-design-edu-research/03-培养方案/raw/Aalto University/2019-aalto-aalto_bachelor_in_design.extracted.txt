Admission process
•
Preliminary assignments
•
Uploaded to the online application by 30 Jan 2019 at 15.00 at the latest
•
Evaluated Pass/Fail
•
Intake assignments
•
Delivered to the applicants who pass the preliminary assignments on 13 March 
2019
•
Submitted to the Admission Services by 26 March
•
Evaluated on the scale of 0-50
•
Interviews
•
Invitations sent to applicants who pass the intake assignments by 18 Apr 2019
•
Interviews are organized 24-29 Apr 2019
•
Evaluated on the scale of 0-50
Admission is based on the total score of intake assignments and interviews, max. 100p
