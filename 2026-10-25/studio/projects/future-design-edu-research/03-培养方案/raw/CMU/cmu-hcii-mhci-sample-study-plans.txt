MHCI Sample Study Plans
Here are sample plans for a full-time student in either the MHCI or the MHCI - Advanced Study program. The MHCI degree is designed to be earned in one year, August through August, in three semesters of on-campus study. The MHCI - Advanced Study degree is designed to be earned in 16 months, August - December of the following year, in four semesters of on-campus study.
There are no remote or online programs or schedules available.
