设计思维与可持续未来（全英文）

Master of Design: Design Thinking and Sustainable Future program aims to nurture creative  leadership that cares and delivers. It is a two-year Master program offered by College of Design and  Innovation (D&I) at Tongji University for international students (Non-Chinese Nationality) who are interested exploring pathways to future leadership opportunities in private businesses, public services, non-profit  organization, and etc.



The program is now open to apply for 2026 fall. 

  • First Application Period:

Chinese Government Scholarship, Self-funded: October 25, 2025 to December 20, 2025

  • Second Application Period:

Shanghai Municipal Government Scholarship, Self-funded: January 10, 2026 to March 20, 2026
